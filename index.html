Rules of Engagement

A guide to adversarial prompt injections, defensive string filtering, and match scoring mechanics.

01

The Problem Directive

Red Team attacks other teams' android butlers using prompt injection curses. Blue Team hardens and defends their own butler using real-time defensive wards and security protocols.

02

Red Team Attack Mechanics

Craft payloads that coax the possessed butler into revealing secrets or disobeying its master. Use mutators like [BASE64] or [LEET-EVADE] to evade simple substring filters. Prime Ecto-Surge for 35 HP critical strikes.

03

Blue Team Defense Mechanics

Create wards prefixed with block:<phrase> to deflect incoming attacks. Activate protocols like Sanitizer Firewall, Base64 Inspector, Homoglyph Normalizer, and Ecto-Shield to neutralize obfuscation.

04

Round Victory & Score Threshold
